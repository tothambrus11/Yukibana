Third-party software in the Rust pack (app/static/packs/rust/)
==============================================================

rust-analyzer.wasm
  rust-analyzer's IDE crates (ra_ap_ide, ra_ap_ide_db, ra_ap_hir and the
  other ra_ap_* crates, version 0.0.358), https://github.com/rust-lang/rust-analyzer
  Copyright the rust-analyzer developers. MIT OR Apache-2.0.
  Their crates.io dependencies (salsa, rowan, chalk/next-solver crates,
  smallvec, hashbrown, serde_json, ...) are MIT and/or Apache-2.0
  (Cargo.lock in packs/rust/server lists them); the Rust standard library
  compiled into it is MIT OR Apache-2.0.

std-src.tar
  core, alloc and std from rust-src 1.98.0, https://github.com/rust-lang/rust
  Copyright the Rust Project Developers. MIT OR Apache-2.0.

miri.wasm, sysroot.tar
  Miri and rustc (1.78.0-dev), compiled to WebAssembly by bjorn3
  (https://github.com/rust-lang/miri/issues/722), and the
  x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu sysroot built with it, as published by rubri
  (https://github.com/LyonSyonII/rubri, MIT) at commit
  00de75bcec3d8cf5156aef2382c738cbfe069986. Miri, rustc and the standard
  library: Copyright the Rust Project Developers, MIT OR Apache-2.0. The
  sysroot's other crates (hashbrown, libc, memchr, object, gimli, addr2line,
  miniz_oxide, adler, cfg-if, rustc-demangle, getopts, unicode-width,
  compiler_builtins) are MIT and/or Apache-2.0 (compiler_builtins also
  carries the LLVM exception).

quickcheck-1.0.3.tar, quickcheck-1.0.3-src.tar
  quickcheck 1.0.3, https://github.com/BurntSushi/quickcheck, Copyright
  Andrew Gallant. Unlicense OR MIT (used here under MIT).
  Its dependencies, from crates.io (versions in packs/rust/crates/Cargo.lock):
  rand 0.8.8 and rand_core 0.6.4 (https://github.com/rust-random/rand,
  Copyright the Rand Project Developers and the Rust Project Developers),
  getrandom 0.2.17 (https://github.com/rust-random/getrandom, Copyright the
  Rand Project Developers), libc 0.2.191 (https://github.com/rust-lang/libc,
  Copyright the Rust Project Developers) and cfg-if 1.0.5
  (https://github.com/rust-lang/cfg-if, Copyright Alex Crichton), each MIT
  OR Apache-2.0. The .tar holds their compiled metadata, the -src.tar their
  sources.

runner.js
  includes browser_wasi_shim 0.4.2, https://github.com/bjorn3/browser_wasi_shim,
  Copyright bjorn3 and contributors, MIT OR Apache-2.0.
